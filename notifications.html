Campus Privacy Workflow
  1. Owner spots item: Clicks Request Contact with proof of ownership.
  2. Finder reviews claim: Reviews identifying details in this Notifications hub.
  3. Finder Approves: Official college email is safely unlocked for both.
  4. Handover: Meet on campus to coordinate the item exchange.
  5. Recovery: Status updated to Returned / Recovered.
